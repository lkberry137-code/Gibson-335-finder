60-Second On-Site Inspection Guide

Pre-handover checks before parting with cash or bank transfer

1 Headstock Fracture / "Smile" Inspection

Run your fingers down the transition between the headstock and neck. Check under strong flashlight or UV for witness lines or overspray. A repaired headstock is an automatic −25% to −35% value reduction.

2 Truss Rod Nut & Thread Life

Authentic Gibson truss rod covers use 2 screws (replicas almost always use 3). Ensure the brass nut is not rounded off, buried beneath the wood, or out of adjustment thread.

3 Neck Relief & Lateral Twist Check

Sight down both edges of the fingerboard from bridge to headstock. Ensure neck relief is even on both treble and bass sides without a ski-jump or corkscrew twist.

4 Fret Nibs & Binding Quality

Original Gibson USA & Orville by Gibson models feature binding nibs covering the fret ends. If the frets go over the binding, it has been refretted (inspect fretwork quality).

5 Bridge Posts & Hardware Spec

Real Gibson ABR-1 or Nashville bridges thread directly into the wood or standard Gibson bushings. Metric bridges with wide flathead screwdriver slots on top indicate counterfeit builds.
